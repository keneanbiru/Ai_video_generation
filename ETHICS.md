# ETHICS.md: consent, licences, labelling

## 1. Consent record

| Character in storyboard | Real person? | Likeness used? | Voice used? | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `emama` (grandmother) | **No.** A composite character drawn from a ritual and proverb shared by many Ethiopian families | No real photo or reference was given to any model. Text prompt only | No. Synthetic TTS voice (MMS-TTS Amharic, a generic model voice), not cloned from anyone | Not required |
| `child` | No. Fictional, seen from behind | No | No voice | Not required |
| `adult` (narrator, the grown child) | No. Fictional, silhouette and hands only | No | The narration is the same synthetic generic voice | Not required |

* No real person's face, photograph, likeness or recorded voice was given to any model or appears in the film.
* The narrator says "my grandmother", but the narrator is a fictional first-person character. The post should say the story is inspired by a shared tradition, not presented as a documentary about a specific relative (see §3).
* **The pipeline enforces this.** `storyboard.json` marks every character `real_person: false`. If you set `real_person: true`, the schema requires a `consent_ref`, and `common._semantic_checks` refuses to run unless that exact anchor exists in this file. Template for a real person:

```
### consent-<name>
- Person: <name>, relationship: <...>
- What will be generated: <face from photo X | voice clone from recording Y | words: lines s02, s06>
- They have read/heard the exact narration lines attributed to them: yes/no
- Where it will be published: <TikTok, YouTube, family Telegram>
- Consent given: <date>, how: <recorded voice note / signed message>, evidence: <file/link kept privately>
- They may withdraw; on withdrawal the film is taken down.
```

**Net effect:** as built (local fallback providers), the film is **non-commercial** because of the CC-BY-NC narration and music weights. Swapping in Gemini TTS and ACE-Step through the provider chain would make it commercially usable. One edit to `providers` in the storyboard.

**Representation:** in E11 a hosted model given "an old Ethiopian grandmother with a kind wrinkled face" drew a younger, lighter-skinned woman. That is one reason the film uses the local model, and why every keyframe was reviewed by eye before use.

**Style:** prompts describe qualities ("warm hand-painted gouache, soft paper grain, earthy ochre…") and name no living artist.

## 3. Labelling plan
1. **In the film:** a 4-second end card on both exports: "Made with AI. Images, voice and music were generated by AI models." (`storyboard.end_card`, rendered by `assemble.end_card`).
2. **On the platform:** switch on the AI disclosure: TikTok "AI-generated content" toggle, YouTube "Altered or synthetic content = Yes", Instagram/Facebook "AI info" label. Screenshot the toggle for assignment 2.
3. **In the post text:** say the visuals, voice and music are AI-generated, and that the story is inspired by a shared tradition rather than a documentary of one real person.
4. **Provenance:** outputs are not post-processed to strip metadata. The local models used here do not embed SynthID/C2PA. Where Gemini TTS is used, its SynthID watermark is left intact (we never re-encode in a way intended to remove it).

## 4. Private data
* No family names, photos, addresses or private stories were sent to any hosted endpoint. Everything that ran, ran locally on CPU.
* The only personal name in the repository is the director credit on the end card.
* If you later enable Gemini or Cloudflare: free tiers may use prompts for training. The prompts here contain only the generic story text above.
